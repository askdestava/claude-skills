Instrumental only, no vocals. A 30-second piece for a short product launch film, modern electronic, confident and warm.

Shape: it opens quiet and sparse, a soft pulse and a low pad that slowly build tension, adding small rhythmic elements
and a rising filter sweep. The build ends in one full beat of complete silence. Then a big, punchy drop: a deep kick,
a wide bass, bright chords, an energetic groove that stays full and steady to the end, with a clean ending.

Keep the build clearly quieter than the drop. Make the drop hit hard and immediately after the silence.
